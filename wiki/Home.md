# Surveyor Map Framework

Surveyor records the world you explore — terrain, structures and waypoints — keeps it with your save, and shares it between players on a server. Map mods such as [Antique Atlas](https://github.com/Darkona/antique-atlas-neoforge/wiki) draw it. Surveyor has no map screen of its own.
